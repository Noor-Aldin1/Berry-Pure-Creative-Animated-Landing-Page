The Berry Pure Story

From a market stall to an artisanal icon

Berry Pure began as a weekend farmers' market stall in Copenhagen in 2012, with one simple, bold idea: take the season's finest fruits and transform them into something extraordinary. No artificial shortcuts, no preservatives — just hand-picked strawberries, sun-ripened bananas, and luscious blueberries blended into bowls and scoops that taste like summer in every bite.

What started as a passion project between two food-obsessed friends, Lena and Erik, quickly grew into a beloved local institution. Word spread not just because of the flavour, but because of the unwavering commitment to quality and the warmth baked into every creation.

Today, every Freshify creation is still made in small batches, handcrafted daily in our kitchen, and served with the same love that started it all. We believe the best desserts are the ones you can feel good about eating — and that is exactly what we make.

What We Stand For

Crafted with purpose, served with love

Every decision we make — from the farm we partner with to the bowl we hand you — is guided by these core principles.

100% Natural Ingredients

We source only certified-natural, seasonal fruits from trusted local farms. Zero artificial flavors, colorings, or preservatives — ever. What you taste is exactly what nature intended.

Handcrafted Every Morning

Every bowl and scoop is made fresh in small batches each morning. We never batch-freeze our product — each serving is prepared on the day it is enjoyed, guaranteeing peak freshness.

Sustainably Sourced

We work exclusively with farms that share our commitment to regenerative growing practices. Our packaging is 100% recyclable, and we offset our carbon footprint through verified climate programs.

Community First

Berry Pure started in our local community and that is where our heart stays. We support local farms, employ from the neighbourhood, and donate 2% of profits to food-security charities.

Innovation Meets Tradition

We honour classic recipes while constantly experimenting with new seasonal combinations. Our test kitchen operates year-round developing limited-edition flavours that surprise and delight.

Uncompromising Quality

Every ingredient passes a rigorous quality check before entering our kitchen. If a fruit isn't at its flavour peak — it doesn't make the cut. We would rather serve less than serve less-than-perfect.

Our Journey

Twelve years of milestones

2012

The First Stall

Lena & Erik set up Berry Pure's first market stall at Copenhagen's Torvehallerne market with just two flavours and a dream.

2015

First Physical Café

Demand grew so fast we opened our first brick-and-mortar café in Nørrebro. On opening day, the queue stretched around the block.

2019

Best Natural Dessert Award

Recognised by the Scandinavian Food Innovation Awards as the region's Best Natural Dessert Brand for three consecutive years.

2024

International Presence

Now serving over 12,000 happy customers across 3 locations in Denmark, with international shipping of our artisan scoops launching 2025.
